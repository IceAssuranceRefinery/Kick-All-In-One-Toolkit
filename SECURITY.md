## Pro Tips
<a id="pro-tips"></a>

- **Use Residential Proxies** — Datacenter proxies trigger Kick's bot detection. Invest in residential or mobile proxies (Smartproxy, Bright Data) for 95%+ connection success.
- **Gradual Viewer Ramp** — Start with 100 viewers and increase by 50-100 every 10 minutes. Instant spikes to 5,000 viewers look suspicious and risk channel suspension.
- **Mix Bot & Organic Chat** — Configure chatbot to respond only to 30-50% of chat messages. 100% bot responses look robotic—let organic viewers fill gaps.
- **Schedule Off-Peak Automation** — Run follow bot and raid campaigns during your offline hours. Automated activity while streaming looks less authentic.
- **Download VODs Immediately** — Kick deletes VODs after 7 days for non-partners. Enable auto-download to archive every stream within 24 hours.
